# CutPlan v1.6.3 verification report

Date: 13 November 2026

## Change delivered

- Fixed the regular-label collision test. The previous helper compared the reference label's vertical coordinate against the vertical dimension label's horizontal coordinate, so a reference could be accepted even when its actual text box collided with the length label.
- Every candidate label is now measured as an axis-aligned bounding box after applying its SVG rotation. The helper rejects candidates that overlap, touch, violate the inner panel padding, or extend outside the panel rectangle.
- Regular panels first try the usual horizontal width label and vertical length label. If either collides, CutPlan tries alternate edge positions and interior positions, then moves/rotates the reference to another free area. If a clean arrangement still cannot be found, it switches to a combined `Length × Width` label inside the panel.
- Small panels continue to use combined `Length × Width` notation whenever either source dimension is strictly below 90 mm. Exactly 90 mm remains regular unless the regular layout needs the combined fallback to avoid a collision.
- The reference retains its previous **10 pt** target. Dimension values retain the **11.5 pt** target when space permits; either can adapt down only when the physical panel cannot contain it safely.
- Tiny/short panels use one compressed in-box line as a last resort. No external part-label schedule or off-panel text is introduced.
- No optimizer geometry, parser, cutting measurements, banding counts, manual panel entry or board/PDF page layout changed.

## Collision coverage

- Text bounding rectangles are checked after rotation in the real browser, not just from estimated text positions.
- Every label must remain within its own part rectangle.
- No pair of labels within the same part may overlap.
- Narrow regular panels, long references, small panels, tiny parts and slivers are included.
- A long reference on a narrow regular panel is moved/rotated to the unused long axis while keeping its 10 pt target.
- Small panels retain combined `Length × Width` notation.
- The generated A4 PDF was rendered and inspected; no external labels are used.

## Verification boundary

Automated checks use deterministic synthetic rows and regression fixtures. Inspect the output at actual print scale against a real workshop cutting list before production cutting.
