# CutPlan v1.6.0 verification report

Date: 13 November 2026

## Change delivered

- Increased the target size of part-reference and dimension labels from 10 pt to **20 pt** (2×), using bold type.
- The renderer no longer progressively shrinks regular edge labels to a smaller font to force them into narrow parts.
- Regular panels (both source dimensions at least 90 mm) keep separate width-aligned and length-aligned labels when all labels fit at 20 pt.
- If the separate edge labels do not fit at 20 pt, the panel uses a horizontal `Length × Width` label at 20 pt. If even that combined label cannot fit, an in-panel marker links to the external part-label schedule.
- Panels with either source dimension below 90 mm continue to use combined `Length × Width` notation.
- Enlarged the external part-label schedule substantially for both screen and print.
- Drawing dimensions remain unit-free; dimensions in the input forms, settings and calculation summaries retain their units.
- No changes to optimizer geometry, parser behavior, row data, edge-banding calculations or cut validation.

## UI and drawing coverage

- Large regular panel `1200 × 480`: 20 pt separate side-aligned labels.
- Small panel `500 × 89`: combined `500 × 89` label rule, with large external schedule fallback when the in-panel text cannot fit.
- Small panel `80 × 180`: combined `80 × 180` rule.
- Boundary panel `600 × 90`: treated as regular (the threshold remains strictly below 90 mm); if its 20 pt side labels cannot fit, it falls back to combined notation.
- The combined fallback is unit-free and remains 20 pt when it fits inside the panel.
- A4 print-media preview confirms the enlarged schedule remains visible.
- Chromium test reports no JavaScript page errors.
